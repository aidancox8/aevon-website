The rules, in short

Which trades are compulsory under BC Skilled Trades Certification?

Seven: Construction Electrician, Industrial Electrician, Powerline Technician, Steamfitter/Pipefitter, Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Mechanic, Gasfitter Class A and Class B, and Sheet Metal Worker. SkilledTradesBC lists Mobile Crane Operator and Tower Crane Operator as taking effect July 5, 2027.

Who can legally do work in a compulsory trade?

A certified journeyperson, a registered apprentice working under the supervision of a certified journeyperson, or a SkilledTradesBC designated Trade Qualifier or Uncertified Experienced Worker.

What is the apprentice supervision ratio?

SkilledTradesBC sets a maximum supervision ratio of two apprentices to every one journeyperson (2:1).

What do employers have to do?

Verify that workers in compulsory trades are registered apprentices, trade qualifiers or certified journeypersons, keep documentation of their qualifications, keep within the 2:1 supervision ratio, and complete the Employer Workforce Submission in the SkilledTradesBC Portal.

How is it enforced?

SkilledTradesBC randomly selects employers for on-premises spot checks, reviewing records such as redacted payroll and certifications, and uses a graduated approach of remediation action plans, written warnings, compliance orders and administrative monetary penalties.

Does this checker send my crew list anywhere?

No. The file is read in your browser and never uploaded. It is a working aid, not legal advice; SkilledTradesBC's requirements are the authority.

Sources: SkilledTradesBC, Skilled Trades Certification and compliance and enforcement. This page is a working aid, not legal advice.
