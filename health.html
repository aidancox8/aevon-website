The problem

Your staff already know what falls through.

None of it is hard. There just isn't time.

The referral that went quiet

A referral goes out and nothing comes back. Someone has to remember to chase it, usually from memory or a spreadsheet, and some slip through.

The slot that stayed empty

A consult cancels the day before. Nobody has an hour to phone down the list, so the slot sits empty while the waitlist runs for months.

The pattern nobody saw

Cancellations, no-shows and rebookings pile up through the week. By the time anyone looks, the week is over.

How it works

  1. We connect to your EMR

    Through its official API, using a dedicated account your clinic creates and controls. No screen scraping and no shared staff logins.

  2. The automation does the checking

    Each day it looks for what you asked it to watch, such as referrals with no response after a number of days you set.

  3. Your staff get a short list

    As tasks inside the EMR they already use. There is nothing new to log into. Anything uncertain goes to a person. The automation does not guess.

What we're building first

Two jobs, done properly.

Both are in development with founding clinics. We would rather do two things well than promise ten.

For family practices

Referral chase list

Each morning, a list of outgoing referrals that have had no response, waiting for your front desk as tasks in the EMR.

  • You set how many days counts as "no response"
  • A short daily summary of cancellations and schedule changes
  • Staff-facing only. Nothing is sent to patients
For specialist clinics

Cancellation backfill

When a slot opens, patients on your own waitlist get a text. The first to say yes is booked into the schedule.

  • Your staff can approve each booking, or let it run
  • Messages contain no health details
  • A monthly count of slots filled

On the roadmap, not yet available: recall and reactivation, no-show follow-up, intake preparation, and WorkSafeBC and ICBC form preparation. Need something else automated? We build custom work too. Tell us what eats your front desk's time.

Questions

Does this replace our EMR or booking tool?

No. It works alongside them. If you use an online booking tool, it keeps doing its job.

Do staff need to learn new software?

No. The chase list shows up as tasks inside your EMR.

Where is patient information stored?

In Canada. We store as little as possible, and your privacy impact assessment lists exactly what is stored and where before anything goes live.

Who can see our data?

Only the people named in your privacy impact assessment, using logged, multi-factor access.

What does it cost?

Pricing is per physician per month, plus a one-time setup fee. Founding clinics start with a 60-day pilot at no charge. You get a number on the call.

What if we want to stop?

You disable the EMR account and access ends that day. We delete your data and confirm in writing.

Is this AI making decisions about patients?

No. These automations follow rules you set and handle administrative follow-up only. Clinical decisions stay with your physicians.
