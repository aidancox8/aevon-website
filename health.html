The problem

Your staff already know what falls through.

None of it is hard. There just isn't time.

The referral that went quiet

A referral goes out and nothing comes back. Chasing it depends on someone remembering, or on a spreadsheet. Some slip through.

The slot that stayed empty

A consult cancels the day before. Nobody has an hour to phone down the waitlist, so the slot goes unused.

The week nobody saw

Cancellations and schedule changes pile up. By the time anyone adds them up, the week is over.

How it works

Three steps. Nothing new to log into.

  1. We connect to your EMR

    Through its official API, using one dedicated account that your clinic creates and controls. No screen scraping and no shared staff logins.

  2. The automation does the checking

    Each day it looks for what you asked it to watch, such as referrals with no response after a number of days you set.

  3. Your staff get a short list

    As tasks inside the EMR. Anything uncertain goes to a person. The automation does not guess.

What we're building first

Two jobs, done properly.

Both are in development. We would rather do two things well than promise ten.

For family practices

Referral chase list

Each morning, outgoing referrals with no response are waiting for your front desk as tasks inside the EMR.

  • You set how many days counts as "no response"
  • A short daily summary of cancellations and schedule changes
  • Staff-facing only. Nothing is sent to patients
For specialist clinics

Cancellation backfill

When a slot opens, patients on your own waitlist get a text. The first to say yes is booked into the schedule.

  • Your staff can approve each booking, or let it run
  • Messages contain no health details
  • A monthly count of slots filled

Questions

Is it available now?

Not yet. Both automations are in development. We are taking on founding clinics to build them with, starting on OSCAR.

Does this replace our EMR or booking tool?

No. It works alongside them. If you use an online booking tool, it keeps doing its job.

Do staff need to learn new software?

No. The chase list shows up as tasks inside your EMR.

Do patients get messages from it?

Only with cancellation backfill, and only patients on your own waitlist. The texts contain no health details, and your staff can approve each booking. The referral chase list is staff-facing only.

Where is patient information stored?

In Canada, and we store as little as possible.

What does it cost?

Pricing is per physician per month, plus a one-time setup fee. Founding clinics start with a 60-day pilot at no charge.

What if we want to stop?

Your clinic disables the one EMR account we use, and our access ends.

Is this AI making decisions about patients?

No. The automations follow rules you set and handle administrative follow-up only. There is no triage, diagnosis or treatment. Clinical decisions stay with your physicians.
