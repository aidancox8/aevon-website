Services

Follow-up booking: assigned in the EMR, booked by text

A doctor assigns the follow-up to Aevon in place of the front desk. The patient picks a time by text or email and the appointment is booked in the EMR.

  • Reads the doctor's own wording, such as RTC 6/52 or f/u 2 wks
  • Offers three open times from the doctor's schedule, never on a holiday or a day the clinic is closed
  • Offers new times when the patient asks, such as mornings only
  • Books the visit, confirms it and closes the task with a note
  • Sends two reminders, then returns the task to your front desk
  • Never decides anything clinical and never books anything urgent

Referral intake: from fax to booked

A new referral goes from a sorted fax to a booked appointment. Exceptions go to your staff.

  • Acknowledges the referring office and asks for what is missing
  • Offers times by text to patients who have agreed to texts, and books the one they pick
  • Gives your staff a task to call when there is no text consent on file
  • Sends the confirmation and welcome email
  • Staff can confirm each booking first, if your clinic wants that

Questions

How does a doctor assign a follow-up?

In the EMR, the doctor creates a task as usual and assigns it to Aevon in place of the front desk. For example: Follow-up in 6 weeks, 15 min. Nothing else changes for the doctor.

What does the patient see?

A text from your clinic. It says it is from an automated assistant, names the doctor and offers three open times. The patient replies 1, 2 or 3, or asks for other times, and gets a confirmation. The messages contain no health details.

What happens when a patient does not reply?

They get two reminders. After that the task goes back to your front desk with a note.

Does it call patients?

No. It uses text and email only. It does not make voice calls.

Which patients does it contact?

Only patients who have agreed to texts or email with your clinic. If there is no consent on file, the task goes to your front desk.

Does it decide anything clinical?

No. It never decides anything clinical and never books anything urgent. A task marked urgent goes to your front desk without a text to the patient. If a patient writes about results, symptoms or medication, the task goes to your front desk with their words quoted.

Does this replace our EMR or booking tool?

No. It works alongside your EMR and does not replace it. If you use an online booking tool, that keeps working the way it does now.

How does a new referral get booked?

If the patient has agreed to texts with your clinic, they are offered times your clinic allows, by text. If there is no consent to text on file, your staff get a task to make the first call instead. When a patient picks a time, a referral that meets all your clinic's rules is booked and the confirmation and welcome email go out. If anything is missing or unusual, it goes to your staff.

What does it cost?

Pricing is per physician per month, plus a one-time setup fee. Clinics start with 30 days at no charge, with free onboarding.

What if we want to stop?

Your clinic disables the one EMR account we use, and our access ends.
