Questions

Does this replace our EMR or booking tool?

No. It works alongside your EMR and does not replace it. If you use an online booking tool, that keeps working the way it does now.

Does it decide which referrals are urgent?

No. It reads what the referring office wrote and follows the rules your clinic sets for provider and appointment type. Urgency is a clinical decision and stays with your physicians.

How does a new referral get booked?

If the patient has agreed to texts with your clinic, they are offered times your clinic allows, by text. If there is no consent to text on file, your staff get a task to make the first call instead. When a patient picks a time, a referral that meets all your clinic's rules is booked and the confirmation and welcome email go out. The service checks the schedule before booking. If anything is missing or unusual, it goes to your staff. Your clinic can also choose to confirm every booking.

Do patients get messages from it?

Only patients who have agreed to texts with your clinic. For booking, they get a short form request, the offered times and the confirmation. The messages contain no health details.

What does it cost?

Pricing is per physician per month, plus a one-time setup fee. Clinics start with 30 days at no charge, with free onboarding.

Your clinic disables the one EMR account we use, and our access ends.
