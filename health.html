Where front desk follow-up gets missed

The two things we are building first both deal with follow-up that is done by hand today.

Referrals with no reply

A referral is sent and the other office does not respond. Finding those means someone keeping a list and checking it, which is hard to keep up on a busy day.

Cancelled appointments

A patient cancels a day or two ahead. Filling the slot means phoning down the waitlist, and there is rarely time for that, so the appointment goes unused.

Schedule changes

Cancellations and changes are spread across the day and across staff. Nobody has a single view of them unless someone puts one together by hand.

How it works

  1. We connect to your EMR

    We use the EMR's official API and one dedicated account that your clinic creates and controls. We do not use screen scraping or shared staff logins.

  2. It checks every day

    The automation looks for the things you have asked it to watch. For example, referrals that have had no response after a number of days that you choose.

  3. Staff see a list of tasks

    The results appear as tasks inside the EMR, so there is nothing new to log into. If the automation is not sure about something, it leaves it for a person to decide.

What we are building first

Both of these are in development and neither is available yet.

For family practices

Referral chase list

Each morning, outgoing referrals that have had no response show up as tasks for your front desk inside the EMR.

  • You choose how many days count as no response
  • It includes a short daily summary of cancellations and schedule changes
  • Only your staff see it. Nothing is sent to patients
For specialist clinics

Cancellation backfill

When an appointment slot opens up, patients on your clinic's own waitlist get a text message. The first patient to reply yes is booked into the schedule.

  • Staff can approve each booking before it is made, or let it run automatically
  • The messages contain no health details
  • You get a monthly count of the slots that were filled

Questions

Is it available now?

Not yet. Both automations are in development. We are looking for founding clinics to build them with, starting with clinics that use OSCAR.

Does this replace our EMR or booking tool?

No. It works alongside your EMR and does not replace it. If you use an online booking tool, that keeps working the way it does now.

Do staff need to learn new software?

No. The chase list appears as tasks inside the EMR your staff already use.

Do patients get messages from it?

Only if you use cancellation backfill, and then only patients on your own waitlist. The texts contain no health details, and your staff can approve each booking. The referral chase list is only seen by staff.

Where is patient information stored?

In Canada, and we store as little as possible.

What does it cost?

Pricing is per physician per month, plus a one-time setup fee. Founding clinics start with a 60-day pilot at no charge.

What if we want to stop?

Your clinic disables the one EMR account we use, and our access ends.

Is this AI making decisions about patients?

No. The automations follow rules that your clinic sets, and they only handle administrative follow-up. They do no triage, diagnosis or treatment. Clinical decisions stay with your physicians.
