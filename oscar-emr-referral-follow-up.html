How to track referrals with no response in OSCAR EMR

Short answer. Open the Consultations screen. It lists every consultation request with its status and referral date, and it shows overdue requests in red. A link on that screen creates a tickler for requests that have sat at "Nothing" for more than a week.

Step by step

  1. Open the Consultations screen. It is reached from the Con link on the top bar, and from the Consultations section of a patient's chart. The screen is titled View Consultation Requests.
  2. Set the filters. Choose a team, set a date range, and choose whether to search on Referral Date or Appointment Date. Leave Include Completed unticked so finished requests stay out of the way.
  3. Read the Status and Referral Date columns. Each row shows the Status, Urgency, Team, Patient, Provider, Service, Consultant, Referral Date, Appointment Date and Last Followup.
  4. Look for the red rows. OSCAR shows a request in red when it is overdue. The table below gives the number of days for each status.
  5. Create ticklers for the ones at "Nothing". Use the link "Add Tickler for Consults with ND for more than one week". It creates a tickler for each of those requests, which then appears in the Tickler Manager.
  6. Update the status when a reply arrives. When the specialist's office confirms or the report comes back, change the request's status. This step is what keeps the list trustworthy.

What the statuses mean

StatusMeaningTurns red after
NothingThe request was created and nothing has happened since7 days
Pending Specialist CallbackSent, waiting to hear from the specialist's office30 days
Pending Patient CallbackWaiting on the patient7 days
Booking ConfirmedThe specialist appointment is bookedDoes not turn red
CompletedFinished. Hidden unless you tick Include CompletedDoes not turn red

Where the manual way falls short

  • Someone has to open the screen. The red rows only help on the days a staff member looks at them.
  • It depends on the statuses being kept up to date. If nobody changes a request's status when the specialist replies, it will show as overdue when it is not.
  • The one-click tickler covers one case. It is for requests at "Nothing" for over a week. Requests waiting on a specialist are flagged in red but need a tickler made by hand.

Questions

Does OSCAR EMR track outgoing referrals?

Yes. Outgoing referrals are consultation requests. The Consultations screen lists them with their Status, Urgency, Team, Patient, Provider, Service, Consultant, Referral Date, Appointment Date and Last Followup.

Why are some consultation rows red in OSCAR?

A row turns red when the request is overdue: more than 7 days at Nothing or Pending Patient Callback, or more than 30 days at Pending Specialist Callback.

Can OSCAR remind staff to follow up a referral?

Partly. The Consultations screen has a link that creates a tickler for requests that have had a status of Nothing for more than a week. Someone has to open the screen and click it. It does not run on a schedule.

What is a tickler in OSCAR?

A tickler is a task or reminder attached to a patient. It has a Service Date, a Priority of High, Normal or Low, the person it is assigned to, and a message. Ticklers are managed in the Tickler Manager.

Is this the same in OSCAR Pro and Juno?

The idea is the same, but the wording differs. Juno, for example, uses the statuses Not Complete, Preliminary Pending Specialist, Pending Callback, Appointment Booked, Completed, Cancelled and Deleted. Check your own version.
