Two categories of software, and the gap between them

If your staff hold tickets that expire, you have probably tried both of these and ended up keeping a spreadsheet anyway.

📋

HR platforms

Treat a certification as a text field. They will store the expiry date, but a custom field cannot trigger a workflow, so nothing happens when it passes. The reminder is still a human remembering.

⚠️

Certification trackers

Do that one job properly and know nothing else about the person. No hiring, no onboarding, no policies, no equipment, no reviews. You maintain the same roster twice and reconcile it by hand.

📊

So: the spreadsheet

The actual compliance answer ends up in a file somebody owns personally, updated when they get to it, and invisible to everyone else until an audit or an incident asks for it.

The renewal loop

This is the part that only works when the credential and the training live in the same system.

01

It sees the expiry coming

Every credential carries a real date and a renewal period, so Cadre knows weeks ahead, not the morning after.

02

It tells the person

By email and Microsoft Teams, where they already are, rather than a portal nobody signs into.

03

It escalates before, not after

If the ticket is still open the manager hears about it while there is time to book the course.

04

Completing the course closes it

The training record and the credential are the same record, so finishing the renewal clears the reminder. No re-keying.

Questions

Can I see it without talking to anyone?

Yes, and you should. The demo is the real application with a sample company loaded, signed in as an HR director. Nothing is a screenshot. Change things and see what happens.

What kinds of company is this for?

Anywhere the employer, rather than the individual, is accountable for the workforce's tickets: trades and construction, manufacturing, transport and fleet, security, food processing, social services, aviation maintenance. Roughly 15 to 400 staff. Below about 15, nobody is accountable for renewals yet and a spreadsheet genuinely is fine.

We already have an HR system. Does this replace it?

It can, and that is usually the point, because running both is how the double data entry starts. If you are locked into payroll elsewhere, that is fine: payroll is the one thing Cadre deliberately does not do.

Does it do payroll?

No, on purpose. Payroll is a solved, regulated, well-served problem and competing with it would make everything else worse. Cadre imports from your payroll system so the employee list stays in step.

How long does setup take?

Days, not a quarter. The work is importing your people and naming your departments, sites and credential types. We do that with you rather than sending a CSV template and wishing you luck.

Who is behind it?
