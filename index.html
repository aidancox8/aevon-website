Our product

Cadre. Certification tracking for trades and transport.

Every ticket on the employee record, with the training that renews it in the same system. Completing the course closes the expiry.

  1. Every credential carries a real date

    Issue, renewal period and expiry, per person and per ticket.

  2. It warns the person, then their supervisor

    Email and Teams, before the date rather than after it.

  3. Completing the course closes the credential

    Training and certification are one record. Nothing is re-keyed.

  4. Who is current, on demand

    By site, ticket or status, as a CSV. An audit is a screen.
