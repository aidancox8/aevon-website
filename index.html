How we work

One product at a time, built properly, sold to people we can actually support. Cadre is what we are shipping now. If a second one earns its place it will be here too, and it will have come from the same thing that produced this one: watching a real company do a job badly with the tools it had.

You deal with the person who writes the software. That is a genuine limit on how many customers we can take, and it is also why the product fits the way these businesses run rather than the way a category is supposed to work.

We publish a working demo of everything we sell, with nothing held back behind a sales process. It is the fastest honest answer to "is this any good", and it is the only kind of proof a company our size has earned the right to offer.
