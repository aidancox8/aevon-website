Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 25, 2026

Aevon ("we", "us", "our") operates the website aevon.ca. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our site or contact us, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

What we collect

We collect information in two ways:

  • Information you provide directly — when you fill out the contact form, we collect your name, email address, and any details you include in your message.
  • Information collected automatically — if we use analytics tools (such as Google Analytics), we may collect standard log data including your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on the site. This data is aggregated and not linked to your identity.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and discuss a potential project
  • To follow up after an initial conversation if we've both agreed to stay in touch
  • To understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies

Our site may use cookies for analytics. These cookies do not identify you personally. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time without affecting your ability to use the site.

Data retention

Contact form submissions are retained only as long as needed to follow up on your inquiry. If a project does not move forward, we delete your information within 90 days.

Your rights

Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA), you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of any personal information we hold about you. To make a request, email us at hello@aevon.ca and we will respond within 30 days.

Third-party services

Our site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google may collect basic request data (such as your IP address) as part of serving those resources. See Google's privacy policy for details.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at hello@aevon.ca.

Aevon is based in the Lower Mainland, BC, Canada.
