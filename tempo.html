What it does

The scheduling your booking software leaves to you

Patient booking tells patients when you're available. Someone still decides who works, where, and when. That is Tempo's whole job.

🗓

Weekly staff + room schedule

Which practitioner is in which room, each day. Auto-filled from your defaults, adjusted in clicks, published to everyone instantly.

🖥

Front desk & support scheduling

Reception, aides, and admin staff live on the same schedule as practitioners, the part patient-booking tools tell you to keep in a separate calendar.

📱

Sick-call cover in one tap

Someone calls out, Tempo finds every qualified staff member who's free and texts them. First yes fills the shift and everyone gets confirmed.

💵

Payroll-synced time off

Approved time off flows into the schedule automatically, and scheduled hours export payroll-ready per pay period.

📊

Room & coverage analytics

Room utilization, coverage per day of week, away impact, evening and weekend load, in your clinic's own vocabulary.

🛡

Credential expiry watch

Registrations, licences, and First Aid expiries tracked per person, with warnings long before anything lapses.
