What you get, and what you don't

Being specific up front so nobody wastes an afternoon. This is deliberately narrow, because a narrow thing that actually works beats a broad thing that half does.

✓ Yes

  • One process, start to finish
  • Built around how you do it, not a template
  • Real and clickable, not a mockup
  • Sample or historical data you send over
  • Yours to keep either way, no strings

✕ No

  • Not a whole system or a suite
  • Not wired into your live customer traffic
  • Nothing that needs a paid service to run
  • No ongoing cost handed to you afterwards
  • Not an obligation to buy anything

If what you need is bigger than one process, tell me anyway. I'll say so honestly and we can talk about scoping the real thing, rather than me pretending a weekend covers it.
